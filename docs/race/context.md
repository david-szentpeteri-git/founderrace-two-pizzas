# Context from the rest of the FounderRace site

Captured Sat 21 Nov 2026 from the English pages of founderrace.com: home, concept, editions, projects, partners, behind the scenes, registration and terms of sale. Local snapshots are in `raw/site/` (not in git).

## What FounderRace is (concept page)

- "The world championship for builders." A SaaS is "an online tool people use straight from their browser, with nothing to install. You can build one yourself in 24 hours, even without knowing how to code."
- Three steps. Before: you sign up, solo or with two teammates, and prepare your tools, not your product. During: the theme is announced at the start, and you have 24 hours to publish a working product and get people to try it. After: "You send your link and a short video. Experts rank the projects; the best ones are rewarded and picked for the Grand Final."
- "To win, you will have to…": 01 identify a real problem (someone has to need it) · 02 build a quality MVP (simple, but it really works) · 03 bring proof of traction (visitors, sign-ups, feedback) · 04 make a video pitch ("Two minutes to present your product") · 05 **set the best time** ("Ship before the others, within the 24 hours").
- Item 05 is not one of the jury's four criteria in the binding rules (Art. 8). Shipping early still earns checkpoints and puts the MVP screenshot on file early.
- The home page adds: "The top three earn their spot in the grand final."

- Cash, €2,000 in total: 1st €1,000, 2nd €600, 3rd €400. Paid by bank transfer to the team captain a few days after the race.

- Best Use of Tavily: 10,000, 5,000 and 3,000 Tavily credits.

## Home page FAQ

- **Who can take part?** Anyone who wants to build, whatever their background: developer, designer, marketer, student, employee, founder. No minimum level. Open from age 16.
- **Are AI and no-code allowed?** Yes, everything is allowed: AI, no-code, public boilerplates. Only what you ship during the 24 hours counts. One limit: the project is born during the race, and any prior work disqualifies.
- **I have no team, can I race?** Yes. The solo track is a real track. Teams have three runners at most.
- **When does Edition 2 take place?** 21 November 2026 at 09:00, Paris time, 100% remote.
- **Does the project remain mine?** Yes. FounderRace may only show your pitch and the name of your project to talk about the race.
- **Where can I see what was built during Edition 1?** In the project gallery and on the Edition 1 page.
