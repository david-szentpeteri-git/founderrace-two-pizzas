## 01 Race day, hour by hour

- **Saturday 21 November · 08:30. The finish line opens.** Sign in with the email you registered with: a 6-digit code reaches you by email. That is where you will submit your deliverables.
- **Saturday 21 November · 09:00. Start.** Live on stream and on Discord: the sub-theme is revealed, the clock starts. You can build.
- **09:00 → Sunday 09:00. 24 hours of racing.** You build, you ship, you go after your first users. Lives throughout the race, each one announced on Discord fifteen minutes ahead.
- **H+2 · H+4 · H+6 · H+8 · H+10 · H+12 · H+14 · H+20. Race updates on Discord.** The race points standings, for the show (see below).
- **Sunday 22 November · 09:00. Finish.** The finish line freezes: what is submitted at that time is what the jury looks at. Nothing is accepted after, not even a minute late.
- **Sunday 22 November · after deliberation. The results.** The podium is announced live on stream and on the official Discord. The top three teams are contacted by the organisers.

All times are Paris time.

## 02 One theme, one sub-theme

- Season theme: **Artificial Intelligence**
- Edition sub-theme: **Human Connection**

**A SaaS, no exceptions.** An online software product that people can use. Not a showcase website, not a service.

**A broad theme, not a brief.** You pick your product freely, as long as it ties in with the theme.

**You can change your idea.** Pivoting during the race is allowed, within the same rules.

## 03 Prepare your tools, not your project

You can:

- **Install your tools**: editor, AI, no-code, whatever you like.
- **Create your accounts**: hosting, domain name, payments, APIs.
- **Pick your stack** and rehearse your moves.
- **Get ready to record your video pitch** at the end of the race.
- **Sleep.** Seriously.

You cannot:

- **Write your product's code** before the start.
- **Draw mockups** or produce content in advance.
- **Reuse an existing project**, even your own, even unfinished.
- **Create your GitHub repository before race day.** A public boilerplate goes in with a commit after the start, and is named in the README.

**Mandatory: at least one partner tool.** During the race, every team uses at least one tool from this list: **Lovable**, **Stripe** and **Tavily**.

**Your partner perks** (credits, codes) are posted on Discord the day before the race: create your accounts that evening so you do not lose a minute.

## 04 You are not alone on the track

**Discord, the official channel.** The sub-theme, the announcements, the race updates and the organisers' answers: everything goes through Discord. Your access link is in your confirmation email.

**The live, 24 hours on air.** The start, lives throughout the race, the results: everything is streamed live, barring technical issues. [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@founderraceofficial/streams) · [Twitch](https://www.twitch.tv/founderrace)

**Go live, +7 points.** Book a short call on Discord, streamed on the live: the control room gives you 7 race points. Optional.

**Your pace, your place.** Sleeping, meeting your team in one place: it is all up to you. There is no official venue.

## 05 Three deliverables, nothing more

1. **Your MVP's address.** Your product live, publicly reachable by the jury. A screenshot is taken for every address you save and at the finish: if your site blocks bots, turn that protection off.
2. **Your video pitch.** In mp4, mov or webm, 500 MB maximum, in French or English: the problem and your solution, your results and traction, a product demo, and a few shots of your race.
3. **Your proof of traction.** A read-only Stripe key (a restricted key): it counts the payments received between the start and the finish. Zero sales is still a valid proof.

**Not taking payments with Stripe?** Submit the read-only key of your payment platform and tell us on Discord or by email as soon as it is done: otherwise your payments cannot be counted.

- You also present your project: its name, its tagline, the problem it solves and its key feature.
- Your GitHub repository is optional. If you give it, it must be public.
- Every team member can submit, and everything can be replaced as many times as you like until the finish. Submit early, improve later.

## 06 Judged on what ships

1. **A real problem.** Someone has to need it.
2. **The quality of the MVP.** Simple, but it really works.
3. **The proof of traction.** What you actually collected and who signed up.
4. **The video pitch.** Your product, your proof, your race.

- Four criteria of equal weight, no numeric scale: an overall judgement, final and without appeal.
- A paying customer weighs more than a signed-up user, who weighs more than a visitor.
- The amount collected counts, not the number of sales: one €10 sale is worth as much as ten €1 sales.
- The jury relies on your video, your live product, your proof of traction and your GitHub repository if you gave it.

## 07 Points, for the show

**Checkpoints to unlock.** In any order you like, measured during the race. Your points show on your finish line.

**Standings on Discord.** The race update drops at H+2, H+4, H+6, H+8, H+10, H+12, H+14, H+20. The standings are also on air during the live.

**Never the jury's score.** Points are there for the show: they do not count in the final ranking. The jury judges on its four criteria.

A point seems to be missing, or something is wrong? Write to Sylvain on Discord or by email.

## 08 €2,000 cash prize, on the podium

1. 1st: €1,000
2. 2nd: €600
3. 3rd: €400

- Teams and solo runners are ranked together: a solo runner can win.
- The results come out on Sunday, live on stream and on the official Discord. The top three teams are contacted by the organisers.
- The prizes are awarded a few days after the race, by bank transfer, to the team captain (or the solo runner).
- Partners add prizes for the podium: the details are on Discord.

## 09 Red card

- **Work done before**: code, mockups, content or an existing project, produced before the start.
- **Changes after the finish**: code pushed or a submission changed after closing is not taken into account.
- **A private or empty GitHub repository** at the finish, when you gave one.
- **Plagiarism**: a copied project, or one that infringes someone's rights.
- **Fake proof**: made-up sales, fake accounts, inflated numbers.
- **Cheating on the grid**: more than 3 people per team, false identity, multiple registrations.
- **Unsporting behaviour**: harassment, sabotage, cheating.

## 10 What everyone asks

**Can I sell my SaaS for €1 to get lots of customers?** You can, but it will not help. The jury looks at the amount actually collected, not the number of sales: one €10 sale is worth as much as ten €1 sales.

**Does a free user count?** Yes, but less than a paying customer. Paying customer, then signed-up user, then visitor: that is the jury's order.

**I do not take payments with Stripe, what do I do?** Submit the read-only key of your payment platform, then tell us on Discord or by email. Otherwise your payments cannot be counted.

**Is GitHub mandatory?** No, it is optional. If you give it, it is created on race day and it is public: a private or empty repository at the finish can get you disqualified.

**How long should the video be?** The rules set no length, only 500 MB maximum. Aim for 1 to 3 minutes: the problem, the product running, your proof, and a few shots of your race.

**What happens at the finish?** Everything freezes. What is submitted at that moment is what the jury looks at: a screenshot of your MVP, your video, your payments. Nothing is accepted after.

**Can I sleep?** Yes. You set your own pace: nobody checks that you are at your screen, only the result counts.

**Can I use a boilerplate or a template?** Yes, if it is public. You add it with a commit after the start and name it in your README.

**Are paid tools allowed?** Yes. AI, no-code, APIs, hosting: everything is allowed, free or paid. One rule: the project is born during the race.

**A teammate does not show up, what do we do?** Your team races without them: their absence does not cancel your entry. A team change is requested from the organisers on Discord, until the start.

**Do race points count for the jury?** No. They are there for the show: the jury judges on its four criteria, without looking at the points.

**Is the project still ours?** Yes. What you build is yours. FounderRace may show your pitch and your project's name to talk about the event.

## 11 A problem on race day?

- **Otherwise: sylvain@founderrace.com.** If Discord is down, or for anything that must stay private.
