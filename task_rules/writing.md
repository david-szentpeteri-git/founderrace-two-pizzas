# Writing rules

Trigger: writing anything people outside the team will read. That covers landing page and in-app copy, the README, the pitch script and captions, outreach messages and social posts, emails to users, and the finish-line project fields. Commit messages follow `CLAUDE.md` (Git).

1. **Sound like a person.** Load the `humanizer-main` skill if it is installed and apply it. If it isn't, follow its patterns directly:
   - no "not X but Y" contrasts, no one-line closers, no staged openers, no forced lists of three;
   - no bold labels on list items; sentence-case headings;
   - none of the stock AI words: delve, tapestry, testament, seamless, leverage, robust, elevate, unlock, empower, harness, streamline, revolutionize, game-changer, cutting-edge, landscape, journey, realm;
   - no inflated claims or sales language. Be plain, specific and honest; the jury values honesty and the rules forbid inflated numbers.
2. **No em dashes or en dashes**, anywhere. Use commas, colons, full stops or parentheses.
3. **Names.** Write "Szentpeteri" without an accent on the e.
4. **Numbers.** Traction figures in any copy or in the pitch must match the real data (Stripe, analytics). Never round up.
5. **In chat**, no raw LaTeX. Write math with unicode symbols, code blocks and tables.
